About & Help

Understand how AI Interviewer works, its workflow, and connect with the creator.

🎯 Detailed Description

AI Interviewer is an intelligent, mock interview preparation platform designed to help students and professionals ace their recruitment assessments. In today's competitive job market, preparation is key. This platform simulates a real-life coding and voice-based interviewing environment, reducing nervousness and strengthening response delivery.

By leveraging advanced AI capability (via Gemini API models), the platform asks targeted technical, coding, and behavioral questions tailored to your experience. Your spoken answers are captured, and the code is evaluated dynamically. In addition, timed analytical tests help you prepare for preliminary rounds.

⚙️ How to Use (Platform Workflow)

1. Configure Settings & Audio Devices

Navigate to the Settings page to select your microphone. Adjust input levels and check volume parameters to ensure your voice response captures smoothly.

2. Choose Your Focus Area

Select either Interview for specialized speech & coding practice, or Aptitude Tests for speed-based multiple-choice quizzes.

3. Conduct the Assessment

For coding interviews, write your solution directly in the integrated IDE. For speech questions, toggle the recording, state your answer aloud, and hit submit. The AI will evaluate your answers instantly.

4. Review Performance & Feedback

Check the Analytics dashboard to view comprehensive report cards. Track your scores, response times, and topic mastery metrics over time.

❓ Questions & Answers (FAQ)

Do I need to enable my microphone?
Yes. Voice mock interviews require microphone access to capture and record your spoken answers, allowing the AI engine to evaluate your vocabulary, coherence, and technical clarity.
How are coding questions evaluated?
The coding workspace includes a full-featured Monaco Code Editor. Once you complete your code, it executes on our secure runtime environment against multiple predefined test cases to verify logic correctness and edge-cases.
What is "Offline Demo Mode"?
If the platform backend server cannot be reached, the system falls back to a mock demo mode. This allows you to inspect the UI and workflow screens with offline data. Starting the local backend via SETUP/START scripts activates the full AI evaluation pipeline.
Are my assessments kept private?
All mock data is securely stored inside your local profile database. We respect user privacy; only your account credentials and metrics are saved to evaluate your analytical trends.
